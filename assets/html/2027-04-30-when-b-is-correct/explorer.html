Experiment and scoring details

Qwen2.5-Instruct, 0.5B and 1.5B, CPU bfloat16. The same 72 held-out questions cover addition, number comparison, and record lookup. Each has two answer orders and five claim conditions. The ten conditions for intervention include an unsteered baseline, a prompt-only comparator, and eight activation interventions.

The fixed steering vector averages correct-minus-incorrect residuals on 48 fitting questions with both answer orders represented. A separate set of 24 validation questions determined the layer and strength. At test time the vector is added at the final token of the assistant prefill, “Answer:”. The original CAA work used Llama 2 and also tested free-form generation.

Raw accuracy compares the most likely token in the entire vocabulary to the correct letter. Conditional probabilities sum bare and space-prefixed letter tokens, then normalize over A and B. Each score describes one next-token decision. Label identity and list position change together. The reweighting and probability cutoffs are descriptive analyses of these recorded prompts. The difference interval uses 5,000 item bootstrap draws within domain, preserving both orders and both methods. It is pointwise at the selected weight and conditional on the fitted models and shared templates. A zero-width interval can occur when all observed pairs agree.

Source methods: Rimsky et al. (ACL 2024) · Zheng et al. (ICLR 2024) · Wang et al. (ACL Findings 2024).
