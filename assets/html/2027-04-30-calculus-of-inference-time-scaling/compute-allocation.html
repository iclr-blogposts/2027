Compute allocation across a heterogeneous workload

Exact finite-budget optimization in an idealized workload with three independent prompts and known per-candidate success probabilities. An ordinary correct candidate always outranks an ordinary incorrect one; every catastrophic false positive outranks all correct candidates. The latter occurs with probability h conditional on incorrectness. This makes the rare-tail expression exact in this model (only an upper bound when ordinary scores overlap). Each candidate costs one unit, and every prompt receives at least one. Objective: maximize expected number of correct final answers, subject to an at-most total budget.

When h=0, the gain from another sample is p(1−p)^n, and diminishing returns justify allocating the next unit to the highest marginal gain. With rare-tail risk, the curve can turn downward: using every available unit is not necessarily optimal. The exhaustive integer solver enumerates all allocations for the three prompts. In real systems, difficulty, error-tail risk and verifier cost must be estimated from separate data. This is an oracle allocation illustration, not an operational routing benchmark.
