Compute-aware stopping under correlated verifier errors

Synthetic experiment: independent candidate correctness p; each of two proxy verifiers has rare incorrect-score tail probability h. Shared tail event probability c·h conditional on incorrect; otherwise independent residual events with probability (1−c)h/(1−ch). Thus each marginal is h and joint tail probability is ch+(1−ch)[(1−c)h/(1−ch)]². The parameter c is a shared-error mixture weight, not a Pearson correlation coefficient. Base score distributions are .6+U(0,.5) for correct, .2+U(0,.5) for ordinary wrong, 1.4+U(0,.2) for tail wrong. Min of two scores is a conservative heuristic; it doubles verification calls.

9 conditions, 2,000 independent validation and 2,000 independent test streams each; 64 candidates per stream (36,000 streams and 2,304,000 candidate pairs total). Per condition and method, select the stopping threshold on validation only to maximize accuracy − .012 × cost. Cost = generated candidates + .25 × verifier evaluations, an illustrative unit cost, not FLOPs or latency. Test thresholds are frozen. All methods within a split share candidate streams. Fixed budgets 4/16/64; adaptive cap 64. No abstention; best observed candidate is returned. Accuracy ± one binomial SE. Threshold policy is condition-specific, not a deployable oracle difficulty estimator.
