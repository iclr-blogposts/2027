Group-relative learning signal and clipping

Binary rewards, independently sampled trajectories for one prompt. Population standard deviation convention; no KL term in the displayed policy gradient. The score-function derivative for a Bernoulli logit is r−p.

Same-group centering is not an unbiased score-function gradient: its expectation is (G−1)/G times the raw gradient in this IID setting. Leave-one-out centering corrects this factor. Standard-deviation normalization changes the estimator again. Zero group variance removes the reward-driven term, not necessarily KL gradients.

Exact finite-group calculation, not trained-LLM results. Clipping illustration uses ε=.2. Reward standardization shown without epsilon for nonzero variance; zero variance maps to zero.
