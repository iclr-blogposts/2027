Training signal, verification and compute allocation

Step-score aggregation

Product is a chain-rule probability only if the factors represent conditional validity given previous validity (or under suitable independence assumptions). Minimum is a bottleneck heuristic; last-step score is not automatically an outcome verifier. A geometric mean changes the objective. Step segmentation changes the score distribution. None is universally optimal.

Compare Lightman et al., Let's Verify Step by Step, and Snell et al., Scaling LLM Test-Time Compute Optimally.
