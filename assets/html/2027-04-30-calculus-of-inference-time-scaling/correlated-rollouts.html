When more rollouts do not mean more independent evidence

Exact two-component dependence model: with probability c, all G binary outcomes equal one shared Bernoulli(p); otherwise draw G IID Bernoulli(p) outcomes. The marginal remains p and pairwise correlation equals c for 0<p<1. This is an illustrative joint distribution, not a measured dependence structure of LLM generations.

Mixed group probability = (1−c)[1−p^G−(1−p)^G]. Oracle coverage = cp+(1−c)[1−(1−p)^G]. Variance of sample mean = p(1−p)[1+(G−1)c]/G. Effective sample size for this variance = G/[1+(G−1)c]. This variance-based ESS does not generally predict tail probabilities. Expected rollout count per mixed group is G/P(mixed) if independently resampling whole groups; not an optimality claim about dynamic sampling.
