Every point of the sphere is ψ = a·e₁ + b·eiγe₂ with a = cos(ϑ/2) and b = sin(ϑ/2): height sets the weights a² and b², and longitude is the relative phase γ. The basis states e₁ and e₂ sit at the poles; choose them with the selector. ψ₀ and ψ₁ can be any two points, and the blue great circle between them is the Fubini-Study geodesic. A(x) = a·e₁(x) and B(x) = b·eiγe₂(x) are the two terms of ψ(x), complex numbers at each x; Δ(x) is the angle between them, and the density is |A(x) + B(x)|² = |A(x)|² + |B(x)|² + 2|A(x)||B(x)| cos Δ(x).
