Both predictors assign the same probability P(B) to starting with B. After A, both split equally between 1 and 2. They differ only after B: the reference assigns probability 0.5 to each token, while the stronger predictor assigns 0.5+d to 1 and 0.5−d to 2. The difference d is controlled by the third slider below.

Because the predictors agree on the first token, token-level guidance leaves P(B) unchanged. Joint guidance can change this first-token probability because it also accounts for the possible continuations.
