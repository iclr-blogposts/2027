Two-key Gram matrix conditioning

For two unit keys with cosine c, the Gram matrix has eigenvalues 1+c and 1−c. Its spectral condition number is (1+|c|)/(1−|c|), for |c|<1. With ridge λ>0, the ratio becomes (1+λ+|c|)/(1+λ−|c|).
